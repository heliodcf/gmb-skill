# Post Calendar Templates & Seasonal Content by Niche

## How to Use
Adapt themes and copy to the specific business. Replace [placeholders].
Recommended minimum frequency: 1 post/week to maintain an activity signal.

**Language rule:** Write posts in the language of the business's target market.
Match the language and cultural context of the audience being served.

---

## SEASONAL CONTENT FRAMEWORK

Rather than hard-coded dates (which vary by country), use this adaptable framework
to plan around the calendar events that matter in your client's market:

### Universal High-Value Periods (all markets)

| Season / Period | Content Angle | Best Post Types |
|----------------|--------------|-----------------|
| New Year (Jan) | Fresh start, resolutions, new offerings | What's New, Offer |
| Valentine's / Love | Gifting, couples, self-care | Offer, What's New |
| Spring / Renewal | New services, seasonal reset | What's New, Product |
| Mother's Day (local date) | Gifting, pampering, family | Offer (high priority) |
| Father's Day (local date) | Gifts, services for men | Offer |
| Summer / Peak Season | High-traffic period for most businesses | Event, Offer |
| Back to School | Relevant for family, kids, education businesses | Offer, What's New |
| Fall / Autumn | Season change, new collections, comfort themes | What's New, Product |
| Major Shopping Event (Black Friday, local equivalent) | Discounts, bundles | Offer (high priority) |
| Holiday Season (Nov–Dec) | Gifting, year-end, gratitude | Offer, Event |

### Market-Specific Key Dates to Research Per Client
- Local public holidays (business closures or special messaging)
- Industry-specific observances (World Health Day for clinics, etc.)
- Local community events (city festivals, sports seasons)
- Competitor promotional periods (don't miss them)

## POST TEMPLATES BY TYPE

### Educational Tip (What's New — high engagement)
```
TYPE: What's New
BODY: Did you know that [relevant fact about the industry]? At [Business Name]
in [City], [how this applies to your service/product]. Questions? We're here!
CTA: Learn More → [link]
IMAGE: Simple infographic or process/result photo
```

### Monthly Offer
```
TYPE: Offer
TITLE: [Offer Name] — [Month or Season]
BODY: This [month/season] at [Business Name]: [offer description with % or value].
Valid through [date]. Don't miss it!
CTA: Get Offer → [link]  |  or: Call Now
DATES: [start date] to [end date]
IMAGE: Branded visual with the offer value prominently displayed
```

### Client Testimonial / Social Proof
```
TYPE: What's New
BODY: "[Real quote from a positive review]" — [First name, optional].
Results like this are what drive us every day at [Business Name] in [City].
Ready to experience it yourself?
CTA: Book → [scheduling link]
IMAGE: Before/after (with permission), or a photo of the service being performed
```

### New Service / Product Launch
```
TYPE: What's New
BODY: New at [Business Name]! Starting [date], we now offer [new service/product]
in [City]. [Main benefit in one sentence]. Be among the first to try it!
CTA: Learn More → [link]
IMAGE: Professional photo of the new service/product
```

### Event Announcement
```
TYPE: Event
TITLE: [Event name]
BODY: Join us at [Business Name] for [event description]! [Date, time, what to
expect, any special offer or giveaway]. Space is limited — reserve your spot!
CTA: Reserve → [link]  |  or: Call Now
DATES: [start date] to [end date]
IMAGE: Event-themed graphic or venue photo
```

### Seasonal / Holiday
```
TYPE: Event (if time-bound) or What's New
BODY: [Holiday/Season] is [coming / here]! Celebrate by treating [who] to
[service/product] at [Business Name] in [City]. [Special offer or angle].
Book now before spots fill up!
CTA: Book → [link]
IMAGE: Themed graphic with product or service featured
```

## COPYWRITING BEST PRACTICES

- **First word:** Start with an action verb or keyword — not "Hi," "Hey," or "We"
- **Mobile truncation:** 150–300 characters display without expanding on mobile
- **Hashtags:** Do NOT use — no SEO benefit on GBP, only adds visual noise
- **Links:** Use trackable URLs (UTM parameters or a link shortener) when possible
- **Images:** Posts with images receive approximately 3× more engagement
- **CTA required:** Never publish a post without a call-to-action button
- **Reuse and recycle:** Posts expire/leave the feed but can be re-published with
  minor updates — build a swipe file of evergreen templates
- **A/B mindset:** Track which post types drive the most CTA clicks — double down
  on what works for each specific business
