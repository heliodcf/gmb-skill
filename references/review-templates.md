# Review Response Templates by Industry

## How to Use
Always adapt: replace [placeholders] with real data. Never copy 100% verbatim to
multiple reviews — personalize at least the opening sentence.
Default tone = warm and human. Adjust formality based on business type and client preference.

**Language rule:** Write responses in the same language the customer used.
If the customer wrote in Spanish, respond in Spanish. Portuguese → Portuguese. Etc.

## POSITIVE RESPONSES — Generic Templates

### ★★★★★ — Excellent Experience
```
[Name], thank you so much for sharing this! We're thrilled that your experience with
[specific service] exceeded your expectations. That's exactly the result [Business Name]
in [City] strives for with every client. We look forward to seeing you again soon!

```

```
that they made a difference during your visit. The whole team at [Business Name] is
```

```
Welcome to [Business Name], [Name]! We're so glad your first experience with us was
a positive one. We can't wait to have you back here in [City]. If you ever have
```

```
[Name], thank you for the feedback and the great rating! We're glad you enjoyed
[positive aspect mentioned]. We take your comment about [improvement point] seriously
— that's how we keep getting better. Hope to see you again soon!
```

## GENERAL BEST PRACTICES

- **Length:** Positives: 3–5 sentences. Negatives: 2–4 sentences.
- **Always include:** Business name + city in positive responses (improves response indexing)
- **Never repeat** the exact same response for two different reviews
- **Ideal response time:** within 48 hours for any review
- **Prohibited tone:** defensive, sarcastic, dismissive, or overly corporate/robotic
- **For negative reviews:** never mention the business name (prevents the review from
  surfacing in brand reputation searches)
- **Multi-language note:** If working with multilingual markets, write the response in
  the same language the reviewer used
