# Detailed Scoring Guide — GBP Audit

## Full Scoring Table (100 pts)

Use this rubric to generate an objective score when auditing any profile.

---

### BLOCK 1: FOUNDATION (25 pts)

#### 1.1 — Core Business Information (10 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Business name correct (no keywords, matches real-world signage) | 2 |
| Address complete and verified | 2 |
| Phone number with area code | 1 |
| Website working (HTTPS) | 2 |
| Hours filled per day of the week | 2 |
| Special hours / holidays configured | 1 |

#### 1.2 — Categories (10 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Primary category: specific and accurate | 6 |
| At least 3 relevant secondary categories | 4 |

#### 1.3 — Attributes (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 5+ attributes filled in accurately | 3 |
| Accessibility attributes present (if applicable) | 2 |

---

### BLOCK 2: CONTENT (25 pts)

#### 2.1 — Description (10 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Description is present | 2 |
| Primary keyword in the first sentence | 3 |
| 600–750 characters | 3 |
| No violations (URLs, promotional language, phone numbers) | 2 |

#### 2.2 — Services / Products (10 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Section populated (minimum 5 items) | 4 |
| Individual descriptions per service/product | 3 |
| Prices included (when applicable) | 3 |

#### 2.3 — Q&A (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| At least 5 questions seeded | 3 |
| All answered by the business owner account | 2 |

---

### BLOCK 3: VISUALS (20 pts)

#### 3.1 — Basic Coverage (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Logo present and in spec (250×250px min, square) | 2 |
| Cover photo present and in spec (1080×608px) | 3 |

#### 3.2 — Volume and Variety (10 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 20+ photos total | 3 |
| Photos covering: exterior, interior, team, service/product | 4 |
| Photo quality (sharp, well-lit, professional-looking) | 3 |

#### 3.3 — Visual Activity (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Photo or post added in the last 7 days | 3 |
| At least 1 video present | 2 |

---

### BLOCK 4: REVIEWS (20 pts)

#### 4.1 — Average Rating (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 4.8★ or above | 5 |
| 4.5★ to 4.7★ | 4 |
| 4.0★ to 4.4★ | 2 |
| Below 4.0★ | 0 |

#### 4.2 — Volume (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 50+ reviews total | 5 |
| 20–49 reviews | 3 |
| 10–19 reviews | 2 |
| Fewer than 10 | 0 |

#### 4.3 — Recency (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 4+ reviews in the last month | 5 |
| 1–3 reviews in the last month | 3 |
| No reviews in the last month | 0 |

#### 4.4 — Response Rate (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 100% of reviews responded to | 5 |
| 80–99% responded | 3 |
| 50–79% responded | 1 |
| Less than 50% responded | 0 |

---

### BLOCK 5: ENGAGEMENT & ACTIVITY (10 pts)

#### 5.1 — Posts (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Post published in the last 7 days | 3 |
| Consistent frequency: at least 1/week in the last month | 2 |

#### 5.2 — Features Activated (5 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Messaging (chat) enabled | 2 |
| Booking / action CTA configured | 2 |
| Profile is Google-verified | 1 |

---

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Status | Interpretation |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| 90–100 | ⭐ 10/10 — Elite | Best-in-class profile. Focus on maintenance. |
| 75–89 | ✅ Very Good | Small gaps. Focus on review volume and posting cadence. |
| 60–74 | ⚠️ Average | Clear opportunities. Prioritize content and reviews. |
| 45–59 | 🔶 Needs Work | Foundation incomplete. Immediate action needed. |
| Below 45 | 🔴 Critical | Profile at risk of invisibility. Rebuild from scratch. |

---

## Pre-Audit Quick Checklist

Before scoring, verify:
- [ ] Is the profile verified by Google?
- [ ] Are there duplicate profiles for the same address?
- [ ] Is the profile suspended or under any restrictions?
- [ ] Is the linked website functional?
- [ ] Does the NAP on GBP match the website and social media?
- [ ] Are there customer or competitor photos harming the profile?
